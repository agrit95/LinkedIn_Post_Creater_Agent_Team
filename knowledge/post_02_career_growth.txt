I rejected a job offer that paid 40% more than my current salary.

Everyone thought I was crazy.

Here's why I did it.

The company had zero learning culture. No internal tech talks. No conference budget. No room to experiment. Just ship features, hit deadlines, repeat.

My current company pays less but gives me 20% time to explore new tech. I've built 3 internal tools using AI agents. I've spoken at 2 conferences. I've grown more in 8 months than I did in 3 years at my previous job.

The highest-paying job isn't always the highest-growth job.

Early in your career, optimize for learning speed, not salary. The salary catches up. Trust me.

Here's my framework:
- Will I learn something new every quarter?
- Will I work with people smarter than me?
- Will I have ownership, not just tasks?

If the answer is no to all three, the money won't matter in 2 years.

Your career is a 30-year game. Don't optimize for the next paycheck. Optimize for the next version of yourself.

What would you have done — taken the money or stayed for growth?
