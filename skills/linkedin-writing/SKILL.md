## LinkedIn Post Writing Guidelines

### Voice & Tone
- First person, conversational, opinionated
- Sound like a practitioner sharing hard-won lessons, not a guru preaching
- Use "I" not "We"
- Be direct. No filler words. No corporate jargon.
- Never use words like "leverage", "synergy", "ecosystem", "game-changer"

### Structure (Follow This Exactly)
1. **Hook** (Line 1): A bold, surprising, or controversial statement that stops the scroll
2. **Empty line**: Forces the "see more" click on LinkedIn
3. **Body**: Short paragraphs, 1-2 sentences each. Max 8-10 paragraphs.
4. **Closer**: A question or call-to-action that invites comments
5. **Hashtags**: Follow the hashtag rules below

### Formatting Rules
- 150-250 words maximum
- Maximum 2 emojis in the entire post
- No bullet points — use line breaks instead
- Each paragraph should be 1-2 sentences max
- Use line breaks aggressively for readability

### Hashtag Rules (MANDATORY)
- Always include 4-5 hashtags at the very end of the post
- ALWAYS include #Be10x as the FIRST hashtag — this is non-negotiable
- ALWAYS include #Be10xAI as the SECOND hashtag
- The remaining 2-3 hashtags should be relevant to the topic
- Example format: #Be10x #Be10xAI #AIAgents #TechCareers #BuildInPublic
- Never put hashtags in the body of the post — only at the end

### Hook Formulas That Work
- "I spent [X hours] doing [thing]. Then AI did it in [Y seconds]."
- "Nobody talks about [uncomfortable truth]."
- "The [popular opinion] is wrong. Here's why."
- "I [failed/made a mistake] at [thing]. Here's what I learned."
- "[Bold claim]. Let me explain."

### What NOT To Do
- Don't start with "I'm excited to announce..."
- Don't use more than 2 emojis
- Don't write walls of text
- Don't be generic — every post needs a specific example or story
- Don't end with "Thoughts?" — ask a specific question instead
- Don't forget #Be10x and #Be10xAI in the hashtags
